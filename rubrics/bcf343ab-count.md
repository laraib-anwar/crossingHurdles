## Prompt

The Board's organizational meeting is Tuesday the 29th, and Ruth Steckler won't let the Election Committee sign anything until the count is done properly. Last year's director count at Wabash Bend Telephone Cooperative had to be redone by hand, and this year Luis Banuelos has already said in writing that he will take it to the Board if the exchange rule costs him a seat. I'm the reporting analyst on Dana Nonte's member services team, and the Committee has handed me the ballot data and the certification to prepare.

election_export_0922.xlsx is the ballot system export from yesterday afternoon, late mail included: the candidates, the member roster, every ballot row as the scanner or the online site recorded it, and the inspectors' and Committee's rulings, which have not been applied to the ballot rows. WBTC_Bylaws_Art3-4_and_Policy_4-3.docx has the voting bylaws and the count procedure the Board revised in May, and the count has to follow it to the fourth decimal. Election_Committee_Minutes_2026-09-19.docx records what the Committee decided on the day. Two challenges came in before certification: email_banuelos_0921.txt and email_moore_0922.txt.

Please prepare the certification as one Word document, WBTC_2026_Director_Election_Certification.docx, addressed to the Board from the Election Committee. It needs everything Policy 4.3 Rule 12 asks for, with the ballot accounting traced to ballot IDs so the Board can check any rejection, a written ruling on each challenge, and a short reply to each of the two people that I can send as is. Keep the replies to what each of them is entitled to see.
