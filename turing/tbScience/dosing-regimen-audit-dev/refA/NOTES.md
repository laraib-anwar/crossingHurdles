# Reference A: independent implementation for dosing-regimen-audit

Written only from `environment/data/REGIMEN_SPEC.md`. The oracle (`solution/`) and the expected answers (`gen/expected/`) were not opened.

Contents of this folder:

- `reference.py`: single file, numpy + scipy only, `python reference.py --regimen in.json --output out.json`. Optional flags `--engine expm|taylor`, `--hscale`, `--order` and `--diag` exist for the self-checks; the default call uses none of them.
- `run_all.sh`: writes `out/<name>.json` for every regimen in `../gen/regimens/` and the SHA-256 of each input used to `inputs.sha256`.
- `out/`: the 28 reports.
- `selfcheck.py` and `selfcheck.json`: the evidence below, raw numbers per regimen.

## Method

**State.** `x = [A1, A_p1, A_p2, T_{1,1..n1}, D_1, T_{2,1..n2}, D_2]` (at most 15 states). `L` is the linear part of the right-hand side: CL, Q/V exchange, transit and absorption. The Michaelis-Menten term acts on A1 only.

**Events.** Every IV bolus, lagged oral arrival (`t_d + tlag`, into `T_{f,1}`, or `D_f` when `n_f = 0`), infusion start and end, query time, the horizon and every cycle boundary is a segment boundary. At a boundary the left limit is recorded first, then impulses are added, then the right limit is recorded, so restarts are exact and right-continuity holds. The infusion rate on a segment is the sum over infusions covering the segment midpoint.

**Linear elimination (Vmax = 0): closed form.** The augmented state `z = [x, AUC, R]` obeys `z' = Â z`, where `Â` holds `L`, a unit column that feeds `R` into A1, and an AUC row `1/V1`. Each segment is `z(t+h) = expm(Â h) z(t)` (scipy.linalg.expm, Padé with scaling and squaring). No diagonalisation is involved, so the Jordan blocks of transit chains (eigenvalue `-ktr` repeated `n_f` times) are exact. The AUC is the augmented component, so it is the exact integral of the exponential. Dense values inside a segment are `expm(Â τ) z(segment start)` evaluated directly at each τ.

**Saturable elimination (Vmax > 0): own Taylor-series integrator.** Order 28, with coefficients generated by automatic differentiation of the right-hand side. With `c_k = X_k[0]/V1` and `w = c/(Km + c)`: `w_k = (c_k - sum_{j=1..k} c_j w_{k-j}) / (Km + c_0)` and `X_{k+1} = (L X_k + δ_{k0} R e1 - Vmax w_k e1)/(k+1)`. The step is `h = min_{k in {27,28}} (tol·s/|X_k|_inf)^{1/k}` with `tol = 1e-17`, `s = max(|X_0|_inf, 1e-6 × largest dose)`, capped at 4 h and clipped to the segment end. The AUC per step is the exact integral of the C polynomial. The step's Taylor polynomial is the dense output. The period-map Jacobian comes from the variational equations, expanded the same way: `Y_{k+1} = (L Y_k - (Vmax/V1) e1 sum_j q_j Y_{k-j}[0,:])/(k+1)`, where `q = Km/(Km+c)^2` is built from the series of `s = 1/(Km + c)`.

**Periodic steady state.** One period in phase `[0, P)` of the maintenance-only regimen continued indefinitely:

- boluses at their offsets;
- oral arrivals at `(o + tlag) mod P`;
- infusion rate on each sub-interval equal to `rate × #{m : o + mP <= φ < o + mP + d}`, which handles infusions longer than the period and infusions that wrap.

The period map `Φ` runs from the left limit at the cycle start to the left limit at the cycle end. The fixed point is found by Newton on `Φ(x) - x`, with the Jacobian propagated analytically: a product of expm blocks when linear, the Taylor variational equations when not.

- Linear: Newton starts at `x = 0`, is exact after one step, and takes one or two polishing steps.
- Saturable: the initial guess is the steady state of the linear model with `CL_eff = CL + Vmax/(Km + c̄)`, where `c̄` solves `CL c̄ + Vmax c̄/(Km + c̄) = bioavailable dose rate`. Newton runs with a positivity backtrack and stops when `|Δx| <= 2e-16 |x|` or the residual is below 1e-15.

`trough` is C at the left limit at the period end.

**Extrema and window crossings.**

1. Each dose-free segment gets nodes at spacing `<= min(0.05 h, 0.25/λmax)`, where `λmax` is the largest |eigenvalue| of `L`, plus `Vmax/(Km V1) + CL/V1` when saturable.
2. At each node, `C' = f_1(x)/V1` and `C'' = (J f)_1/V1` are computed from the ODE right-hand side.
3. A sign change of `C'` is bracketed and solved by Brent on the exact `C'(t)`, then polished by one Newton step with `C''`.
4. A sign change of `C''` without one in `C'` triggers a search for the extremum of `C'`. If `C'` changes sign there, both roots are solved.
5. Candidates are the post-impulse value at each segment start (so the post-bolus value counts), the left limit at each segment end (so infusion-end kinks and the closed horizon end count), and the interior critical points. `cmax`/`t_cmax` is the maximum over candidates on `[0, horizon]`. Steady-state `cmax`/`cmin` use the candidates of one period; `cmin` includes the pre-bolus left limits.
6. Between consecutive candidates C is monotone, so each window level has at most one root per piece, found by Brent and polished by Newton with the exact `C'`.
7. `time_in_window` is the total length of the sub-pieces whose midpoint lies in `[low, high]`.

**n_cycles_to_steady.** The actual regimen (loading doses plus maintenance continued without end) is simulated event by event from 0 to `start + Kd P`, where `Kd = max(K0, ceil((horizon - start)/P))` and `K0 = max(2, max_d floor((o_d + span_d)/P) + 1)`. Here `span` is `tlag` for oral doses, the duration for infusions and 0 for boluses. From cycle `K0` on, every cycle's event pattern equals the periodic one, and all loading events (`<= start + 2 h`) fall in cycle 1. Troughs for `m <= Kd` are the left limits at `start + mP`; for `m = Kd+1..60` they come from iterating `Φ`. The answer is the smallest `n` such that every `m` in `[n, 60]` satisfies `|trough_m - trough| <= 0.01 trough`; guarantee 4 covers `m >= 60`.

**AUC** is from 0 to the horizon for the actual regimen, loading doses included.

**Further checks.** Each one is independent of a different part of the code:

- **Direct 60-cycle simulation** (independent of the period map). All 60 troughs were recomputed by plain event-by-event simulation, with no period map and no `K0` logic. The worst relative difference from the reported troughs is 3.0e-13. This validates the wrap-around and overlap handling in held_05, held_09 and held_16 (infusions spanning up to 2.3 periods) and held_11 (a lagged oral arrival wrapping into the next cycle).
- **External integrator** (independent of my propagators). scipy DOP853 at rtol 1e-13, integrated event to event, for the query concentrations and the AUC: worst 3.2e-11 / S and 1.5e-13 relative. That is at DOP853's own accuracy, well inside the 1e-8 tolerance.
- **Brute-force grid scan** (independent of root finding and extremum logic). The steady-state period was scanned at h <= 0.005 h, with bolus times added to the grid, and `[0, horizon]` likewise. Results:
  - The reported SS cmax is never below the grid maximum, and SS cmin never above the grid minimum, beyond rounding (worst -3.3e-14 and -1.4e-13 of SS cmax).
  - The count of continuous level crossings found by root finding equals the grid's sign-change count in all 28 regimens.
  - `|time_in_window - grid estimate|` is at most 0.42 × (grid step × number of crossings), which is consistent with grid resolution.
  - `cmax` is never below the grid maximum, and `t_cmax` is within 0.0022 h of the grid argmax.
- **Determinism.** The `out/` files are reproduced exactly by fresh runs: yes, all 28.

## Spec ambiguities and points to confirm against the oracle

1. **Guarantee 3, which `cmax` sets the crossing slope.** The rule "`|dC/dt| >= 1e-3 cmax` per h" holds in every regimen if `cmax` means the steady-state cmax. It fails if it means the report cmax, which is inflated by loading doses, in: held_08 (slope/report cmax 4.7e-04, slope/SS cmax 1.8e-03), held_09 (slope/report cmax 8.6e-04, slope/SS cmax 1.8e-02). Since guarantee 3 concerns the periodic solution, I read it as the SS cmax, but the wording should name it.

   A related point: a crossing-time error is roughly ΔC/|C'|. A solver whose concentrations sit right at the 1e-8 S tolerance could therefore be off in `time_in_window` by about 1e-8 S / |C'|. For held_08 that is about 2e-05 h, which is more than the 1e-6 h `time_in_window` tolerance. So the time tolerance is the binding one there. The reference itself is about 1e-13 h, so this is only a note on tolerance design.

2. **SS `cmin` includes pre-bolus left limits.** C is right-continuous, so just before a bolus the minimum is not attained. I report the infimum, i.e. the pre-dose value. It matters wherever a bolus sits mid-period and the pre-dose value is the lowest point. Example: held_10, where cmin = 20.70758337 is the pre-bolus value at offset 6 h and the trough is 23.80151023. An implementation that ignores left limits would differ. The spec says "smallest concentration"; the infimum reading is the only well-defined one.

3. **`trough` follows the literal definition even when it is the peak.** In model_06 an infusion ends exactly at the cycle boundary, so SS trough = SS cmax = 25.71652973. That is correct by the spec (left limit at the period end) but unusual for a quantity called a trough.

4. **`cmax` at the closed right end.** In held_03 and held_19, `t_cmax` equals the horizon. In held_03 a maintenance infusion (63 to 67 h) ends exactly at the 67 h horizon. In held_19 (oral only) C is still rising during absorption at the 29 h horizon. The spec's "closed end of the horizon counts" covers both, but they are boundary maxima with `C' > 0` from the left. Guarantee 2's curvature clause does not apply, so any implementation that searches only for interior critical points and dose times will miss them.

5. **Guarantee 2, "local maximum including the post-bolus values".** I counted every post-bolus value as a local maximum, even when absorption keeps C rising after the bolus. All regimens pass even under that strict reading; the tightest is held_22 at 0.999849.

6. **Guarantee 3, "differ by at least 1e-3 relative".** The denominator is not stated. I used `max(level, value)`. The smallest margins are 0.0163 (levels against SS extrema, held_05) and 0.00201 (pre/post-bolus values against levels, held_20), so no reasonable choice of denominator changes the verdict.

## Limitations

- The Taylor step is set by the fastest local rate: roughly 2.8/λ per step at order 28. The regimens here have λ up to about 20 /h, at most 7001 Taylor steps per call (held_15), and run in a few seconds. The domain corner Vmax = 500, Km = 0.05, V1 = 3 gives a Michaelis-Menten rate of 3333 /h at low C, which would need steps near 1e-3 h and could exceed the time limit over 60 long cycles. No shipped or held-out regimen is near it. The linear path's cost does not depend on stiffness.
- Critical-point detection relies on a node grid with spacing `<= 0.25/λmax` and `<= 0.05 h`, plus the `C''` sign test. Two critical points closer than one node spacing, with no `C''` sign change between nodes, would be missed. The grid scan found no such case.
